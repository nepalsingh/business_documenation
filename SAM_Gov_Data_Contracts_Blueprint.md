## Phase 1: Complete the "All Awards" Registration
Since you already have a UEI, you likely have an initial account. However, you must ensure you have completed the **"All Awards" registration** rather than just a basic ID registration, which is required to legally bid on and win contracts.

* **Log in:** Go to the official, free [SAM.gov Portal](https://sam.gov/).
* **Update Profile:** Navigate to your Workspace, select your entity, and choose **"Complete Full Registration"**.
* **Financial Details:** Input your banking routing and account numbers for Automated Clearing House (ACH) direct deposits.
* **IRS Alignment:** Ensure your legal business name and physical address match your IRS Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN/EIN) exactly to prevent automated fraud-detection rejections.
* **CAGE Code:** Submit the registration to automatically trigger your **Commercial and Government Entity (CAGE) Code** assignment.
* **Reps & Certs:** Complete the online "Representations and Certifications" regarding your operational compliance and Section 889 telecommunications restrictions.

## Phase 2: Optimize Your Profile with Target Identifiers
Contracting officers do not just post contracts; they actively search SAM.gov to find qualified vendors for direct, non-competitive "sole-source" awards or small business set-asides. Federal contracting relies on **NAICS codes** (Industry Classification) and **PSC codes** (Product/Service Classification). Make sure your profile explicitly includes these target identifiers.

### Primary NAICS Codes to Add
* **541519** – Other Computer Related Services *(Highly used for general data analytics and tech support)*
* **518210** – Computing Infrastructure, Data Processing, and Hosting Services *(Best for massive data entry or data cloud-cleansing)*
* **541611** – Administrative Management and General Management Consulting Services *(Best for business intelligence and executive dashboard reporting)*
* **561410** – Document Preparation Services *(Best for straightforward, manual data entry and transcription projects)*
* **541910** – Marketing Research and Public Opinion Polling *(Often used for data metrics and statistical reporting)*

### Primary PSC Codes to Add
* **DF01** – IT and Telecom - IT Management Support Services (Labor)
* **R612** – Support - Administrative: Information Retrieval
* **R702** – Support - Management: Data Collection
* **R710** – Support - Management: Financial Management *(Used heavily for budget/spending analytics)*
* **DA01** – IT and Telecom - Business Application Support Services (Labor)

### Claim Set-Asides & Keywords
* **Socio-Economic Certifications:** Self-certify your small business status based on SBA size metrics. If eligible, formally apply for socio-economic certifications (e.g., 8(a), HUBZone, WOSB, SDVOSB).
* **Write Keywords:** Craft a highly detailed capability narrative in your profile using industry-specific search terms that a procurement officer would type into the system.

## Phase 3: Setup Automated Monitoring and Sourcing
Searching for contracts manually every day is inefficient. Do not use generic text searches like "data entry" on the homepage, as they return thousands of irrelevant results. Instead, construct a precise filter query within the **Contract Opportunities** workspace.

### Step-by-Step Search Engine Setup
1. Navigate to **"Contract Opportunities"** from the [SAM.gov Homepage](https://sam.gov/).
2. Click on **"Advanced Search"**.
3. Under the **NAICS Code / PSC Code** filter boxes, enter your targeted data codes.
4. Scroll down to the **Keyword Search** field and input specific strings wrapped in quotes to find precise technical deliverables:
   * `"data visualization" OR "Power BI" OR "Tableau"`
   * `"dashboard reporting" OR "automated reporting"`
   * `"data entry" OR "database management" OR "transcription"`
5. Under **Notice Type**, select **Sources Sought** and **Presolicitation**. Target these *before* they become live "Solicitations" so you can influence the contract requirements and build agency familiarity.
6. Click **"Save Search"** and toggle email notifications to receive daily updates when matching drops occur.

## Phase 4: Market Research & Qualifying Bids
When an attractive solicitation lands in your inbox, run it through a strict vetting and intelligence process before dedicating resources to a proposal.

* **Analyze Past Data via DataBank:** Use the [SAM.gov DataBank / Contract Award Data tool](https://sam.gov/contract-data) to run standard reports on past wins. Search for your target NAICS codes to identify **which federal agencies buy the most data services**, what prices they historically pay, and which large prime contractors win them.
* **Target High-Volume Agencies:** Prioritize agencies like the **Department of Veterans Affairs (VA)**, **Department of Health and Human Services (HHS)**, and the **Social Security Administration (SSA)**, as they routinely outsource massive data workloads.
* **Review Deadlines:** Check the proposal submission deadline, requirements, and amendments meticulously twice weekly per active solicitation to avoid disqualification.
* **Engage Collaborations:** Look at the "Vendor Collaboration" tab on the solicitation page to find prime contractors looking for small business subcontractors.

## Phase 5: Build a Tailored Capability Statement
Before bidding on a live solicitation, you need a one-page business resume (PDF) to email to Agency Small Business Liaison Officers. Ensure it highlights:

* **Core Competencies:** Explicitly detail your software stack (e.g., Python, SQL, Excel, AWS, Tableau, Power BI).
* **Data Security & Compliance:** State whether your systems are HIPAA compliant, or if your personnel are clearable (Public Trust / Secret clearances). Federal data handling requires strict protocol adherence.
* **Differentiators:** Highlight what makes your data entry or reporting faster or more accurate (e.g., Dual-pass verification, custom automated validation scripts).
* **Company Identifiers:** Clear placement of your UEI, CAGE code, and active small business certifications.

---

## Phase 6: Maintain System Compliance
A lapse in registration can freeze active contract payments or disqualify an active bid instantly.

* **Annual Renewal:** Set a recurring calendar reminder to renew your registration every **365 days**.
* **Security Standards:** Ensure your company remains compliant with evolving federal technical requirements, such as the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) standards.
