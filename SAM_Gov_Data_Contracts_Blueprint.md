# Blueprint: Winning Data Entry, Analytics & Reporting Contracts via SAM.gov

This document serves as an operational blueprint for navigating and securing federal government contracts within the **Data Entry, Data Analytics, and Reporting** sectors. Use this guide to optimize your entity profile, configure search matrices, and target high-probability agencies.

---

## 📋 Phase 1: Target Identifiers (NAICS & PSC Codes)

Federal procurement software filters vendors primarily through Industry (NAICS) and Product/Service (PSC) classification codes. Ensure your SAM.gov "All Awards" registration profile explicitly contains the following identifiers:

### 1. Core NAICS Codes
* **541519** – Other Computer Related Services *(Primary bucket for general analytics, cloud visualization, and technical support)*
* **518210** – Computing Infrastructure, Data Processing, and Hosting Services *(Best for high-volume data processing, cloud ingestion, and data cleansing)*
* **541611** – Administrative Management and General Management Consulting Services *(Utilized for Business Intelligence (BI), KPI tracking, and executive dashboard reporting)*
* **561410** – Document Preparation Services *(Best for manual data entry, back-office digitizing, and transcription projects)*
* **541910** – Marketing Research and Public Opinion Polling *(Often used for statistical processing and metrics reporting)*

### 2. Core PSC Codes
* **DF01** – IT and Telecom - IT Management Support Services (Labor)
* **R612** – Support - Administrative: Information Retrieval
* **R702** – Support - Management: Data Collection
* **R710** – Support - Management: Financial Management *(Target this for budget, spending, and financial analytics)*
* **DA01** – IT and Telecom - Business Application Support Services (Labor)

## 🔍 Phase 2: Advanced Search Engine Optimization

Do not rely on the generic search bar on the SAM.gov homepage. Construct a precise logic filter within the **Contract Opportunities** workspace to capture high-intent requests before your competitors find them.

### Step-by-Step Search Construction:
1. Log in to [SAM.gov](https://sam.gov/) and navigate to the **Contract Opportunities** domain.
2. Select **Advanced Search**.
3. Locate the **NAICS Code / PSC Code** filter options and add all the identifiers listed in Phase 1.
4. Navigate to the **Keyword Search** field and input precise Boolean strings enclosed in quotes:
   * For Analytics: `"data visualization" OR "Power BI" OR "Tableau" OR "dashboard reporting"`
   * For Data Entry: `"data entry" OR "database management" OR "transcription" OR "data extraction"`
5. Scroll down to the **Notice Type** filter and select **Sources Sought** and **Presolicitation**. 
   * *Strategy:* Responding to Sources Sought notices allows you to influence the final contract requirements and request that the procurement be set aside exclusively for small businesses.
6. Click **Save Search** at the top right of your workspace and enable **Daily Email Notifications**.

## 📊 Phase 3: DataBank Intelligence & Target Agencies

The General Services Administration (GSA) tracks all historical spending. Use this system to identify who buys what you sell, how often, and at what pricing threshold.

### Strategic Actions:
* Access the [SAM.gov DataBank / Contract Award Data tool](https://sam.gov/contract-data) to pull historical award reports for your selected NAICS codes.
* Identify and monitor the primary target agencies for data-heavy administrative workflows:
  * **Department of Veterans Affairs (VA):** High volume of electronic health record data entry and reporting.
  * **Department of Health and Human Services (HHS):** Constant need for public health data analytics and database management.
  * **Social Security Administration (SSA):** Regular, large-scale administrative record updates and transcription requirements.
* Analyze past contract winners to locate **Large Prime Contractors**. If a contract is too big to bid on alone, reach out to these primes to propose yourself as a niche subcontractor.

## 📄 Phase 4: Capability Statement Blueprint

Before engaging with Agency Small Business Liaison Officers (OSDBU), you must compile a one-page capability statement (business resume) saved in PDF format. Ensure it explicitly details:

* **Core Competencies:** Explicitly list your technical stack (e.g., Python, SQL, Advanced Excel, AWS, Tableau, Power BI, SAS).
* **Data Security & Compliance:** Highlight specific security protocols you adhere to (e.g., HIPAA compliance, NIST SP 800-171, SOC 2). State if your personnel hold active Public Trust or Secret clearances.
* **Corporate Differentiators:** Define what makes your delivery exceptional. Examples include dual-pass verification for 99.9% data accuracy, custom automated validation scripts, or rapid scalability turnarounds.
* **Company Data:** Clearly display your Unique Entity ID (UEI), CAGE Code, and any eligible socio-economic certifications (e.g., WOSB, SDVOSB, HUBZone, 8(a)).

## 🔒 Phase 5: System Maintenance

* **365-Day Renewal:** Set a recurring calendar reminder 30 days prior to your expiration date. If your registration lapses, active contract payments freeze instantly.
* **Profile Verification:** Ensure your banking details (ACH) and IRS tax profiles remain perfectly aligned with your active business registration to avoid automated fraud alerts.
