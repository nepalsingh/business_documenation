# Federal Micro-Purchases: Data Services & System Management Guide

This comprehensive guide outlines how small businesses can leverage the federal government's fast-track micro-purchase program to win IT infrastructure, system management, and data service contracts.

---

## 💰 Federal Micro-Purchase Thresholds (2026)
Governed by **FAR Subpart 13.2**, micro-purchases allow agency buyers to purchase commercial goods and services instantly without traditional competitive bidding.

* **\$15,000** – Standard threshold for commercial goods and data/IT services.
* **\$2,500** – Services subject to the Service Contract Act.
* **\$2,000** – Construction services subject to the Davis-Bacon Act.
* **\$20,000 to \$35,000+** – Elevated limits for contingency operations or disaster relief.

### Key Benefits
* **No Competitive Bidding Required:** Buyers can award contracts directly if prices are reasonable.
* **Paid Instantly via Credit Card:** Government Purchase Cards (GPC) transfer funds directly to your business account within days.
* **Minimal Regulatory Friction:** Bypasses complex proposals, source selection boards, or oral presentations.

## ⚙️ Target Industry Classifications (NAICS Codes)
When marketing to government agencies, ensure your business capability statement references these specific **North American Industry Classification System (NAICS)** codes:

* **541511** – Custom Computer Programming Services
* **541512** – Computer Systems Design Services
* **541519** – Other Computer Related Services (Includes Disaster Recovery and Software Installation)
* **518210** – Computing Infrastructure, Data Processing, and Hosting Services

## 🏛️ Top Federal Target Agencies

### 1. Department of Defense (DoD)
* **Focus Areas:** Localized system health checks, software tweaks, data ingestion scripts, and command-level dashboard builds.
* **Key Targets:** National Guard branches, local Air Force bases, and regional logistics hubs.

### 2. General Services Administration (GSA)
* **Focus Areas:** Cloud architecture optimization, legacy data cleanup, and modernization pilots.
* **Key Targets:** Federal Acquisition Service (FAS) tech offices and regional GSA hubs.

### 3. Department of Homeland Security (DHS)
* **Focus Areas:** Real-time data tracking pipelines and rapid monitoring system scale-ups during emergency management.
* **Key Targets:** FEMA regional centers and border protection technical support units.

## ✉️ Direct Outreach Script
*Note: Because micro-purchases fall below \$25,000, they are **never** posted to SAM.gov. You must email or call Agency Small Business Specialists (OSBUs) and IT Program Managers directly.*

```text
Subject: Micro-Purchase Eligible: Data Services & System Management Support for [Agency Name]

Dear [Contact Name],

I am reaching out from [Your Company Name] (UEI: [Your UEI Number] / NAICS: [Your Primary NAICS, e.g., 541512]). We provide agile data services and system management solutions tailored to simplify complex IT architectures.

Recognizing that your team frequently faces immediate, small-scale technical bottlenecks, we have structured our core capabilities to support rapid-response execution under the \$15,000 micro-purchase threshold. This allows you to bypass lengthy procurement timelines and secure instant support.

Our Core Micro-Purchase Capabilities Include:
* Data Services: Legacy data cleansing, custom API development, and data migration.
* System Management: Real-time performance monitoring, cloud infrastructure optimization, and database troubleshooting.

We are registered and fully active in SAM.gov. I have attached our one-page Capability Statement for your records. 

Are you available for a brief, 5-minute call this week to discuss how we can assist with your team's immediate IT bottlenecks?

Best regards,

[Your Name]  
[Your Title]  
[Your Company Name]  
[Your Phone Number]  
[Link to Your Website]  
```

## 🚀 Execution Checklist
1. [ ] **Register in SAM.gov:** Verify your Unique Entity ID (UEI) is fully active.
2. [ ] **Design a Capability Statement:** Build a 1-page technical marketing PDF.
3. [ ] **Extract Target Lists:** Scrape agency directories for Small Business Liaisons.
4. [ ] **Launch Cold Outreach:** Send 10–15 targeted, micro-purchase focused emails weekly.
